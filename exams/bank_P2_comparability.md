### Q01 [R][Domain I] — Q5E goal
Under ICH Q5E, what must a comparability exercise allow the sponsor to conclude about the pre-change and post-change product?

A. That the post-change product is identical to the pre-change product on release testing.
B. That the products are highly similar and differences have no adverse impact on safety or efficacy.
C. That the post-change lots meet the approved specifications, which itself establishes comparability.
D. That clinical efficacy has been re-established in a bridging trial before the change is implemented.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: Q5E의 목표는 변경 전후 제품이 **highly similar**하고, 기존 지식으로 **안전·효능에 부정적 영향이 없다**고 결론하는 것이다.
- A: comparable은 identical이 아니다. 차이가 있어도 임상 결과에 영향이 없으면 된다.
- C: Q5E는 **규격 충족만으로는 불충분**하다고 명시한다. 규격은 넓어서 추세·분포 변화를 못 본다.
- D: 임상 bridging은 분석·비임상으로 부족할 때의 마지막 단계다. 기본 요건이 아니다.

### Q02 [R][Domain I] — stepwise approach
Which sequence reflects the stepwise approach to comparability described in ICH Q5E?

A. Nonclinical toxicology first, because animal data predict clinical relevance better than analytics do.
B. Quality comparison first; nonclinical, then clinical data where analytics cannot predict clinical impact.
C. Analytical and clinical studies in parallel, with nonclinical data reserved for gene therapy products.
D. Clinical bridging first, then nonclinical studies, then an analytical comparison to confirm the result.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: Q5E는 ① **품질(분석) 비교** → ② 분석이 임상 관련성을 예측 못 하면 **비임상** → ③ 그래도 부족하면 **임상 bridging** 순서다. 분석에서 차이가 없거나 설명 가능하면 거기서 끝난다.
- A: 비임상은 분석이 부족할 때 쓰는 두 번째 단계다.
- C: 임상을 처음부터 병행하는 구조가 아니고, 비임상을 유전자치료제로 한정하지도 않는다.
- D: 순서가 거꾸로다. 분석 비교가 출발점이다.

### Q03 [A][Domain I] — specifications are not enough
A biologics sponsor changes its purification resin. All three post-change lots pass every release specification, and the team proposes to close the comparability exercise on that basis alone. What is the best response?

A. Sufficient, because release specifications are set to capture each attribute relevant to clinical outcome.
B. Sufficient if a fourth lot also passes, since Q5E sets four lots as the minimum for concluding comparability.
C. Insufficient, because any purification change must go directly to a clinical bridging study under Q5E.
D. Insufficient: Q5E requires extended characterization, stability and process data in a side-by-side comparison.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 규격 통과는 필요조건일 뿐이다. Q5E 비교는 출하 시험에 더해 **확장 특성화**(orthogonal 방법), **공정 중 통제·공정 성능**, **안정성**을 변경 전 로트와 **side-by-side**로 본다.
- A: 규격은 넓고 추세·분포 변화를 못 본다. 그래서 Q5E가 불충분하다고 명시한다.
- B: 교과서 기준은 전후 각 **≥3로트(정당화)** 다. 로트 하나를 더한다고 규격 통과의 한계가 해결되지 않는다.
- C: 임상 bridging은 분석·비임상으로 결론 못 낼 때의 단계다. 공정 변경이라고 바로 가지 않는다.

### Q04 [R][Domain I] — evidence factors
Under ICH Q5E, which of the following is NOT a factor that determines how much comparability evidence is needed?

A. The sensitivity and selectivity of the available analytical methods.
B. The extent of product knowledge, including structure-function understanding.
C. The projected commercial sales volume of the post-change product.
D. The stage of development at which the change is made.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: 판매 규모는 증거량 결정 요소가 아니다.
- A: **분석 능력**(감도·선택성)은 요소다. 이 밖에 변경의 범위·성격, 안정성 영향이 있다.
- B: **제품 지식**(구조-기능·CQA 이해)은 요소다.
- D: **개발 단계**는 요소다. 초기에는 임상 데이터로 보완할 수 있고 승인 후는 엄격하다.

### Q05 [R][Domain II] — Q5E adoption
Which statement about the adoption of ICH Q5E is correct?

A. It reached Step 4 in 2004, but the EU replaced it with CHMP 2007, which now governs EU comparability.
B. It reached Step 4 in 2004, the EU adopted it as CPMP/ICH/5721/03 and FDA issued it as guidance in 2005.
C. It reached Step 4 in 2005, and FDA adopted it in 2004 ahead of the other ICH regions as an early adopter.
D. It reached Step 4 in 2004 and applies to postapproval changes, leaving development changes to Q12.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: Q5E는 **2004-11** Step 4, EU는 **CPMP/ICH/5721/03**, FDA는 **2005** guidance로 채택했다.
- A: CHMP 2007은 비임상·임상 comparability를 다루는 보완 가이드라인이다. Q5E를 대체하지 않는다.
- C: 연도가 뒤바뀌었다.
- D: Q5E는 **개발 중과 승인 후 모두** 적용된다.

### Q06 [R][Domain II] — 601.12 map
Which mapping of 21 CFR 601.12 is correct?

A. (b) CBE-30 or CBE-0; (c) PAS; (d) comparability protocol; (e) annual report.
B. (b) PAS; (c) comparability protocol; (d) CBE-30 or CBE-0; (e) annual report.
C. (b) PAS; (c) CBE-30 or CBE-0; (d) annual report; (e) comparability protocol.
D. (b) annual report; (c) PAS; (d) CBE-30 or CBE-0; (e) comparability protocol.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: 601.12 **(b) PAS**(major), **(c) CBE-30·CBE-0**(moderate), **(d) annual report**(minor), **(e) comparability protocol**이다.

### Q07 [A][Domain II] — effect of an approved comparability protocol
A BLA holder obtains FDA approval of a comparability protocol, via PAS, for a planned change that would otherwise require a PAS. The change is later executed and all protocol acceptance criteria are met. How is the change reported?

A. In the annual report, because an approved protocol removes the change from FDA reporting.
B. As another PAS, because the protocol approval covered the study plan, not the resulting data.
C. As a CBE-0 supplement, because the protocol lowers reporting by two categories.
D. As a CBE-30 supplement, one category below the usual category for the change.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 601.12(e)는 시험·합격 기준을 **사전 PAS로 승인**받으면 실행 데이터를 **한 단계 낮게** 보고하게 한다. PAS → **CBE-30**, CBE-30 → AR이다.
- A: 한 단계 하향이다. PAS 변경이 AR까지 내려가지 않는다.
- B: protocol을 사전 승인받는 목적이 실행 시 카테고리를 낮추는 것이다.
- C: 두 단계 하향은 없다.

### Q10 [An][Domain I] — 2026 CBER statement
Before its pivotal trial, an allogeneic MSC sponsor moves from 2D flasks to a 3D bioreactor. Citing the CBER CMC flexibility statement of 2026-01-11, the CMC lead proposes a limited data set instead of a full comparability package. Evaluate.

A. Acceptable, because the statement reclassified bioreactor changes as minor for products in clinical development.
B. Acceptable, because the statement lets sponsors in clinical development defer comparability until the BLA stage.
C. Not acceptable, because the statement applies to postapproval changes rather than to clinical development.
D. Not acceptable: the statement eases burden only for minor changes; this major change needs a full package.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 2026 성명은 임상 단계 **경미 변경**에 과도한 comparability를 요구하지 않겠다는 것이다. 2D → 3D 바이오리액터는 부착·전단·산소·배지 교환이 바뀌는 **주요 변경**이라 완전 패키지가 필요하다.

- B: comparability를 BLA까지 미루게 해 주는 내용이 아니다. 피벗 사용 전 312.31로 제출한다.
- C: 성명은 임상 개발 중 변경을 다룬다(승인 후는 규격 재평가).

### Q11 [A][Domain III] — 312.31 timing
During a Phase 1 IND, a sponsor of an MSC-EV product changes the composition of the cell culture medium. The change could affect product quality. When must the sponsor submit the information to FDA?

A. In the next IND annual report, after the post-change lots have been used in the trial.
B. Within 30 days after first dosing with the post-change product, together with comparability data.
C. As a CBE-30 supplement, with dosing allowed to start 30 days after FDA receipt.
D. As a 312.31 information amendment before the post-change product is used in the trial.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: IND 단계 CMC 변경은 **312.31 information amendment**다. 품질에 영향 줄 수 있는 변경은 **시험에 사용하기 전** 제출한다(GT CMC 2020·Cell CMC 2008).
- A: 품질 영향 변경을 사용 후 연차보고로 돌릴 수 없다.
- B: 사용 후 보고가 아니라 사용 전 제출이다.
- C: CBE-30은 승인된 BLA의 601.12 카테고리다. IND에는 쓰지 않는다.

### Q12 [R][Domain III] — FDA handling of 312.31 amendments
Which statement about a CMC information amendment under 21 CFR 312.31 is correct?

A. There is no FDA review clock, but FDA can place the study on clinical hold if concerned.
B. Amendments are reviewed at the next end-of-phase meeting rather than on submission.

D. FDA must issue written approval within 30 days before the sponsor may use the changed product.

**해설**

- C: 60일 간주 수용 규정은 없다.
- D: FDA 승인 절차가 아니다. 30일은 BLA의 CBE-30 개념이다.

### Q13 [A][Domain IV] — new site, US
A licensed allogeneic macrophage cell therapy will add a new drug product manufacturing facility. No comparability protocol was approved in advance. Which US reporting category applies?

A. Annual report, because a site addition is administrative if equipment is like-for-like.

- D: CBE-30은 사전 comparability protocol이 승인된 경우 실행 데이터를 보고하는 카테고리다.

### Q14 [A][Domain IV] — shelf-life extension, US
A BLA holder for a biological product wants to extend the shelf life. Full shelf-life real-time data are available on three production lots, and the extension follows the stability protocol approved in the BLA. How is this reported in the US?

A. Prior approval supplement, because any shelf-life extension changes the approved labeling.
B. Annual report, provided a comparability protocol was approved for the extension beforehand.
C. CBE-30, because the data come from production lots rather than from pilot lots.
D. Annual report, as described in the 2021 FDA guidance on certain biological products.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: **승인된 안정성 프로토콜**에 따라 **실생산 ≥3로트**의 전체 유효기간 데이터로 연장하면 **AR**이다(2021 FDA *Certain Biological Products* guidance). 프로토콜 밖이면 **PAS**다.

- C: 실생산 로트 데이터는 AR 조건의 일부다. CBE-30으로 올라가지 않는다.

### Q16 [A][Domain IV] — media supplier change
A licensed macrophage cell therapy makes two raw-material changes: (1) it moves its cytokine to a new supplier with the identical composition and specification; (2) it replaces serum-containing medium with a serum-free medium. Which US categories apply?

### Q18 [A][Domain IV] — scale-up beyond the validated range
After approval, an MSC-EV manufacturer moves conditioned-medium production to a new, larger bioreactor that is outside the validated scale range. Which US and EU categories apply?

A. US PAS and EU Type IB, because the EU treats scale changes as minor for biologics.
B. US CBE-30 and EU Type IB, because the process steps and specifications stay the same.

### Q19 [A][Domain IV] — new master cell bank
A licensed allogeneic cell product must establish a new master cell bank from a new donor. Which handling is most consistent with the textbook?

A. US PAS supported by release data alone; EU Type IB, because cell banks are starting materials.
B. US CBE-30 with release data; EU Type IB, because the process itself is unchanged.
C. US annual report if the new bank meets the old bank specifications; EU Type IA.
D. US PAS with extensive comparability, sometimes clinical data; EU Type II.

**정답:** D

**해설**
