### Q01 [R][Domain III] — what counts as labeling
Under FD&C Act 201(m), which item is labeling rather than advertising?

A. A radio spot that names the product and directs listeners to a toll-free number.
B. A sales brochure that a representative hands to physicians alongside the product.
C. A television commercial for the product broadcast during a national news programme.
D. A print advertisement placed in a peer-reviewed medical journal for prescribers.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: 201(m) labeling은 제품에 부착되거나 **동반(accompanying)** 하는 모든 문서다. 영업사원이 건네는 브로슈어는 promotional labeling이다.
- A·C·D: TV·저널·라디오는 labeling 밖의 매체로, 광고(advertising)로 분류된다.

### Q02 [A][Domain III] — PI versus brief summary
A company prepares a detail aid for representatives and a journal advertisement for the same Rx product. The team asks what risk information format each needs. Which answer is correct?

A. Both need full prescribing information, because both are promotional materials reviewed by OPDP.
B. Both need a brief summary, because 502(n) is read to cover labeling and advertising alike.
C. The detail aid needs full prescribing information; the journal advertisement needs a brief summary.
D. The detail aid needs a brief summary; the journal advertisement needs full prescribing information.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: detail aid는 promotional labeling이라 **201.100(d)** 에 따라 **full PI**를 동봉하고, 저널 광고는 **502(n)** 에 따라 **brief summary**를 싣는다.
- A: 광고에 full PI를 요구하는 규정은 없다. 광고의 요건은 brief summary다.
- B: 502(n)은 **광고** 조항이다. labeling에는 201.100(d)가 걸린다.
- D: 두 형식이 뒤바뀌었다.

### Q03 [R][Domain III] — 502(n) contents
Which set of elements does FD&C Act 502(n) require in a prescription drug advertisement?

A. Proprietary name, full prescribing information and the date the advertisement was last revised.
B. Established name, quantitative composition, a brief summary and other FDA-required information.
C. Established name, price, and a statement that the product is dispensed on prescription.
D. Quantitative composition, the approval date and a copy of the Form 2253 transmittal sent to FDA.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: 502(n)은 처방약 광고에 **확립명·정량 조성·brief summary**와 FDA 규정(202.1)이 정한 정보를 요구한다.
- A: full PI는 labeling의 요건(201.100(d))이고, 작성·개정일은 EU Art 92 요건이다.
- C: 가격 표시는 502(n) 요건이 아니다. 가격은 reminder ad에서 넣을 수 있는 항목이다.
- D: 2253은 FDA에 내는 제출 서식이지 광고에 싣는 내용이 아니다.

### Q04 [R][Domain I] — who regulates OTC advertising
Which agency has primary jurisdiction over advertising of over-the-counter drugs in the United States?

A. The Federal Trade Commission, under a 1971 memorandum of understanding signed with FDA.
B. FDA, through OPDP, under the same 202.1 rules that apply to prescription drug advertising.
C. FDA, through APLB, because OTC monographs are administered by CBER rather than CDER.
D. CMS, because OTC drug claims affect federal health programme spending on self-care.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 처방약 광고·labeling은 FDA, **OTC 광고는 FTC** 소관이다(**1971 MOU**).
- B: 202.1은 처방약 광고 규정이다.
- C: APLB는 CBER 생물의약품 판촉 검토 부서다. OTC 광고를 맡지 않는다.
- D: CMS는 판촉 광고 관할 기관이 아니다.

### Q05 [R][Domain III] — omission as misleading
A promotional piece contains only accurate statements but leaves out a material fact about the product's risks. Under the FD&C Act, how is it treated?

A. It is lawful if a brief summary appears anywhere in the same publication issue.
B. It is misleading under 201(n), so the drug is misbranded under 502(a) despite accurate statements.
C. It is treated as a labeling matter, because omissions cannot make an advertisement misleading.
D. It is lawful, because misbranding under 502(a) requires an affirmatively false statement.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: **201(n)** 은 중요 사실의 **누락**도 misleading으로 본다. 그러면 **502(a)** 허위·오도로 misbranded가 된다.
- A: brief summary가 있다고 본문 주장의 누락·불균형이 해소되지 않는다.
- C: 201(n)·502(a)는 labeling과 광고 모두에 걸린다.
- D: 502(a)는 허위뿐 아니라 오도도 포함하고, 201(n)이 누락을 오도에 넣는다.

### Q06 [R][Domain III] — fair balance
In 21 CFR 202.1, what does "fair balance" require?

A. That efficacy claims be supported by two adequate and well-controlled head-to-head trials.
B. That the full list of PI risks be read aloud in any broadcast advertisement for the product.
C. Comparable weight and prominence for risk information relative to efficacy claims in the piece.
D. That risk information occupy the same number of words as the benefit claims in the piece.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: fair balance는 효능 정보와 위험 정보의 **비중·현저성 균형**이다.
- A: head-to-head 근거는 202.1(e)(6)의 비교 우위 주장 요건이다. fair balance의 정의가 아니다.
- B: 방송에서 구두로 요구되는 것은 **major statement**(주요 위험)다. PI의 모든 위험이 아니다.
- D: 단어 수 일치 같은 정량 기준은 fair balance의 정의에 없다.

### Q07 [A][Domain III] — broadcast rule
A company plans a television advertisement for an Rx product and does not want to display the full brief summary on screen. Under 202.1(e)(1), what must the advertisement contain instead?

A. A reminder-format presentation naming the product without any indication or efficacy claim.
B. A major statement of the principal risks plus adequate provision for access to the full PI.
C. A spoken statement that the product is FDA-approved plus a website address for the PI.
D. A major statement of risks, with the brief summary submitted to FDA on Form 2253 instead.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: 202.1(e)(1) 방송 광고는 주요 위험을 구두로 말하는 **major statement**와 brief summary를 대신하는 **adequate provision**(PI 접근 수단)을 갖춘다.
- A: reminder 형식이면 효능 주장을 할 수 없다. 효능을 말하는 광고의 해법이 아니다.
- C: 웹 주소는 adequate provision의 한 요소일 뿐이고, major statement가 빠졌다.
- D: 2253 제출은 광고 안의 정보 요건을 대신하지 않는다.

Which combination reflects the four-element practice for adequate provision in a broadcast Rx advertisement?

A. Referral to a healthcare provider, website, Form 2253 reference number, and a printed patient leaflet.

C. Toll-free number, website, reference to a concurrent print ad, and referral to a healthcare provider.

### Q09 [An][Domain I] — adequate provision status on the reference date
FDA has stated its intention to remove the "adequate provision" route, with a proposed rule targeted for December 2026. For an exam set on 1 September 2026, how should a broadcast Rx advertisement be assessed?

A. Under current 202.1(e)(1), because the proposed rule had not been published by the reference date.
B. As requiring the full brief summary on screen, because FDA's stated intent took effect when announced.

D. Under the CCN rule alone, because adequate provision was withdrawn when the CCN rule itself took effect.

**해설**

- C: 메모는 집행 지시이고 502(n)을 바꾸지 못한다. DTC는 기준일에 합법이다.
- D: CCN은 major statement의 제시 방식 규칙이다. adequate provision을 없애지 않았다.

### Q14 [A][Domain IV] — reminder ad with boxed warning
After approval, marketing proposes a reminder advertisement that shows only the product name and price. The product's labeling carries a boxed warning. What is the correct advice?

A. Not acceptable in print, but acceptable as a broadcast reminder with a spoken major statement.

### Q15 [A][Domain III] — help-seeking next to a product ad
A company runs an unbranded disease-awareness spot that urges viewers to see a doctor about a condition. In the same commercial break it runs its branded reminder ad for the only product it sells for that condition. How will FDA view the pair?

A. As a single product advertisement, so indication and risk requirements apply to the whole.
B. As two separate communications: the help-seeking spot falls outside the advertising rules.
C. As compliant, because neither spot alone contains both the product name and the indication.

- D: coming soon은 승인 전 관행 예외다. 승인 제품의 조합과 관계없다.

### Q16 [R][Domain II] — 312.7 and coming-soon
Which statement about 21 CFR 312.7 and "coming soon" communications is correct?

A. 312.7 permits pre-approval efficacy claims if they are limited to published Phase 3 results.

- D: 312.7은 신청 제출 시점과 무관하게 investigational 제품 전반에 걸린다.

### Q17 [A][Domain II] — coming-soon bundled with disease awareness
Six months before expected approval, marketing wants a coming-soon ad naming the product and company, placed in the same magazine spread as an unbranded awareness ad about the target disease. What should regulatory advise?

### Q18 [R][Domain II] — Form 2253 timing
For a product approved under the standard pathway, when must promotional labeling and advertisements be submitted on Form FDA 2253?

A. At least 30 days before first dissemination, so that FDA can pre-clear it.
B. Within 15 working days of first use, with the annual report.
C. At initial dissemination or publication, as a postmarketing submission.
D. When OPDP requests the material after a complaint or a Bad Ad Program report.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: 2253은 **최초 배포·게재 시점에(동시)** 제출한다. 사전 승인이 아닌 사후 감시다(314.81(b)(3)(i)).
- A: 30일 전 사전 제출은 **accelerated approval** 제품(120일 이후 자료)의 예외이고, 그 경우에도 FDA의 사전 승인(pre-clearance)은 아니다.
- B: 15 working days는 untitled letter·WL 응답 관행이다.
- D: 요청이 없어도 모든 판촉 자료를 제출한다.

Which regulations require submission of promotional materials on Form 2253 for NDA, BLA and ANDA holders respectively?

### Q20 [A][Domain II] — AA launch materials
A product will receive accelerated approval under 314.550. The company has launch materials it intends to use during the first 120 days after approval. When must they be submitted to FDA?

B. 16 March, 30 days before first publication.
