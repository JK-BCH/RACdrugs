### Q01 [R][Domain I] — Fast Track qualifying criteria
Which set of criteria qualifies a drug for Fast Track designation under FD&C 506(b)?

A. A serious condition and data showing potential to address an unmet medical need, or QIDP status.
B. A serious condition and clinical data on a surrogate endpoint reasonably likely to predict clinical benefit.
C. A serious condition and preliminary clinical evidence of substantial improvement over available therapy.
D. A rare condition affecting a small population and completion of at least one adequate Phase 2 study.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: Fast Track(506(b))은 **serious condition** + **unmet medical need를 다룰 잠재력**이 요건이고, 그 근거는 비임상 또는 임상 데이터 어느 쪽이든 된다. **QIDP**도 적격이다.
- B: surrogate endpoint는 accelerated approval(506(c))의 승인 근거다. 지정 요건이 아니다.
- C: 예비 임상 증거로 기존 치료 대비 substantial improvement는 Breakthrough(506(a)) 요건이다.
- D: 희귀성이나 Phase 2 완료는 Fast Track 요건이 아니다.

### Q02 [R][Domain I] — Fast Track benefits
Which benefit is a recognised feature of Fast Track designation?

A. Approval based on a surrogate endpoint without a requirement for confirmatory trials afterwards.
B. Rolling review, in which completed sections of the application are submitted before the rest.
C. A 6-month review goal that applies once the designation has been granted during the IND.
D. Confirmation of clinical benefit through registries and real-world evidence under 506(g)(7).

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: Fast Track 혜택은 FDA와의 **잦은 소통**, **rolling review**, priority review 적격 검토다.
- A: surrogate endpoint 승인은 accelerated approval이고, 확증 의무가 붙는다.
- C: 6개월 심사는 priority review의 결과다. Fast Track이 심사 기간을 정하지 않는다.
- D: 506(g)(7)의 RWE·등록부 확증 유연성은 RMAT 지정의 혜택이다.

### Q03 [R][Domain I] — Breakthrough criteria
What evidence must support a request for Breakthrough Therapy designation?

A. Preliminary clinical evidence of substantial improvement over available therapy on a clinically significant endpoint.
B. Completed Phase 3 data showing a statistically significant effect on a validated clinical endpoint.
C. Preliminary clinical evidence that a cell or tissue-engineered product could address an unmet medical need in a serious condition.
D. Nonclinical data showing a mechanism that could address an unmet need in a serious condition.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: Breakthrough(506(a))는 serious condition + **preliminary clinical evidence**가 기존 치료 대비 **substantial improvement**(임상적으로 중요한 종점)를 시사해야 한다.
- B: Breakthrough는 예비 증거로 요청한다. Phase 3 완료를 기다리면 혜택이 사라진다.
- C: 재생의학 제품의 unmet need 잠재력은 RMAT(506(g)) 요건이다.
- D: 비임상 데이터로 잠재력을 보이는 것은 Fast Track 문턱이다.

### Q04 [R][Domain I] — what Breakthrough adds
Beyond the benefits of Fast Track, what does Breakthrough Therapy designation provide?

A. A shortened statutory review clock for the marketing application and a waiver of the user fee for the original application.
B. Acceptance of a single adequate trial in place of substantial evidence and exemption from the 505(d) standard.
C. A transferable voucher for priority review that the sponsor may sell to another company for a different product.
D. Intensive FDA guidance on efficient development from Phase 1 and organisational commitment involving senior managers.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: BTD는 Fast Track 혜택 전부에 **집중 가이던스**(Phase 1부터 효율적 개발 설계)와 **조직적 commitment**(senior 관여)를 더한다.
- A: 심사 기간은 priority review 문제다. 수수료 면제는 교과서상 BTD 혜택이 아니다.
- B: 지정은 505(d) 증거 기준을 바꾸지 않는다.
- C: 양도 가능한 쿠폰은 PRV다. BTD 혜택이 아니다.

### Q05 [A][Domain II] — FDA response time
A sponsor submits a request for Fast Track, Breakthrough or RMAT designation. Within what period does FDA respond?

A. 60 calendar days for Fast Track, 90 for Breakthrough and RMAT.
B. 30 calendar days for each of the three designations.
C. 60 calendar days for each of the three designations.
D. 90 calendar days for each of the three designations.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: Fast Track·Breakthrough·RMAT 요청에 대한 FDA 응답은 셋 다 **60 calendar days**다.
- A: 지정 종류에 따라 응답 기간이 갈리지 않는다.
- B: 30일은 이 지정들의 응답 기간이 아니다.
- D: 90일은 PRV 사용 통지 기간이다. 지정 응답과 다르다.

### Q06 [A][Domain III] — Breakthrough request after EOP2
A sponsor completed its End-of-Phase 2 meeting six months ago and has started Phase 3. It now wants to request Breakthrough Therapy designation based on its Phase 2 results. What is the best advice?

A. The request should be filed with the NDA, because FDA assesses Breakthrough at the filing review.
B. The request must wait until Phase 3 data are available, because Breakthrough rests on completed trials.
C. The request is late; Breakthrough should be requested before the EOP2 meeting to use its benefits.
D. The request is timely, because Breakthrough may be requested at any point before the marketing application.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: BTD 요청은 **EOP2 전**이 기준이다. 그 뒤에는 집중 가이던스의 의미가 크게 줄어든다.
- A: filing 시점에 판단하는 것은 priority review다.
- B: BTD는 preliminary clinical evidence로 요청한다. Phase 3 결과를 기다리지 않는다.
- D: BTD에 "신청 전 언제든"이라는 기준은 없다. Fast Track의 pre-NDA 기준과도 혼동한 것이다.

### Q07 [A][Domain III] — Fast Track after pre-NDA
Clinical operations proposes requesting Fast Track designation after the pre-NDA meeting "to speed up review." How should regulatory respond?

A. Agree; Fast Track may be requested at any time, and FDA grants it whenever the statutory criteria are met.
B. Agree; Fast Track granted at this stage shortens the PDUFA goal from 10 months to 6 months.
C. Advise that Fast Track must be requested together with the NDA so that rolling review can start.
D. Advise that it is late and may be denied; review speed depends on priority review decided at filing.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: FDA는 Fast Track 요청을 **pre-NDA/BLA 전**에 받기를 권한다. 그 뒤엔 소통·rolling review 혜택이 의미가 없어 거절될 수 있다. 심사 속도는 filing 때 판단하는 **priority review** 문제다.
- A: 요건을 충족해도 pre-NDA 이후 요청은 거절될 수 있다.
- B: Fast Track은 PDUFA 목표 기간을 바꾸지 않는다.
- C: Fast Track 요청은 IND에 제출한다. NDA와 함께 내는 것이 아니다.

### Q08 [An][Domain I] — evidentiary threshold
A sponsor has strong animal efficacy data and a plausible mechanism for a serious disease with no approved therapy. No human data exist yet. Which assessment of its designation options is correct?

A. It may qualify for Fast Track, because potential to address an unmet need can rest on nonclinical data.
B. It qualifies for neither, because both Fast Track and Breakthrough require preliminary clinical evidence.
C. It may qualify for Breakthrough, because animal data can show substantial improvement over available therapy.
D. It may qualify for RMAT, because RMAT is the designation intended for products without clinical data.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: Fast Track의 문턱은 **"잠재력"** 이라 비임상 데이터만으로도 가능하다.
- B: Fast Track은 임상 증거를 요구하지 않는다.
- C: BTD는 **preliminary clinical evidence**가 필요하다. 동물 데이터로는 요청할 수 없다.
- D: RMAT도 preliminary clinical evidence를 요구하고, 재생의학 제품에 한정된다.

### Q09 [R][Domain I] — Breakthrough rescission
Why may FDA rescind a Breakthrough Therapy designation?

A. Because the sponsor received a priority review voucher and both benefits cannot apply to one product.
B. Because the designation lapses once the sponsor submits its marketing application under rolling review.
C. Because the designation must be renewed each year and the sponsor did not file the annual renewal request on time.
D. Because the designation reflects a provisional judgement on current evidence that later data may not support.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: BTD는 **현 시점 증거에 기반한 잠정 판단**이다. 후속 데이터가 substantial improvement를 지지하지 않으면 FDA가 철회(rescind)한다.
- A: 지정과 PRV는 독립적이고 함께 쓸 수 있다.
- B: 신청서 제출로 지정이 소멸한다는 규정은 없다.
- C: BTD에는 연간 갱신 절차가 없다. 1년 갱신은 EU conditional MA 규칙이다.

### Q10 [A][Domain IV] — Fast Track rescission
A product with Fast Track designation loses its basis when another therapy for the same serious condition is approved and the unmet need no longer exists. Which statement is correct?

A. Fast Track is converted automatically into Breakthrough, because the product now competes with available therapy.
B. Fast Track cannot be rescinded; once granted it remains in place until the marketing application is approved.

- B: 철회 가능성은 BTD만의 것이 아니다.

### Q11 [A][Domain IV] — consequence of losing a designation
A sponsor's Breakthrough designation is rescinded after Phase 3 shows a smaller benefit than Phase 2 suggested. The sponsor still plans to submit its NDA. What does the rescission mean for the application?

A. The NDA will be evaluated under the usual 505(d) standard; rescission does not mean refusal.
B. The NDA must wait one year before submission, because a rescinded product needs new Phase 3 data.
C. The NDA must be converted to an accelerated approval application with confirmatory obligations.
D. The NDA will be refused for filing, because rescission shows that substantial evidence is lacking.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 지정이 철회돼도 NDA는 **505(d)** 표준 증거로 평가된다. 철회는 승인 거절을 뜻하지 않는다.
- B: 철회 뒤 제출을 미루라는 규정은 없다.
- C: AA 여부는 종점과 데이터로 정한다. 지정 철회가 승인 방식을 바꾸지 않는다.
- D: 지정 상실과 filing 판단은 별개다.

### Q12 [A][Domain III] — Fast Track is not a review clock
A colleague says, "We got Fast Track, so our NDA will be reviewed in 6 months." Which correction is accurate?

A. Incorrect; Fast Track products are reviewed in 10 months, and only Breakthrough products get 6 months.
B. Correct as stated, because Fast Track products receive priority review once the application is accepted.
C. Incorrect; the 6 months comes from priority review, decided separately at filing on its own criterion.
D. Partly correct; the 6 months applies, but it counts from the receipt date rather than the filing date.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: Fast Track은 개발 단계 지정이라 심사 기간을 자동으로 줄이지 않는다. 6개월은 filing 때 **significant improvement** 기준으로 따로 판단하는 **priority review**의 결과다. Fast Track 제품은 priority 적격성이 검토될 뿐 보장되지 않는다.
- A: BTD도 심사 기간을 정하지 않는다. 기간은 priority 여부로 갈린다.
- B: Fast Track이 priority review를 보장하지 않는다.
- D: 기간의 기산점 문제가 아니라 근거가 틀렸다.

### Q13 [An][Domain IV] — rolling review and the PDUFA clock
Why does rolling review help a Fast Track sponsor, and what limits it under PDUFA?

A. FDA starts the PDUFA clock at the first section, but the sponsor must finish submitting within the 60-day window.
B. FDA reviews each section on its own clock, so the action date is set by the last section's separate deadline.
C. FDA can start reviewing completed sections early, and the PDUFA clock starts when the first completed section is submitted.
D. FDA can start reviewing completed sections early, but the PDUFA clock starts when the final section is submitted.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: rolling review는 완성된 섹션(CMC·비임상)을 먼저 내서 FDA가 **심사를 일찍 시작**하게 한다. 그러나 **PDUFA 시계는 완전한 신청(마지막 섹션) 제출 때 시작**한다. FDA 사전 동의가 필요하고 부분 제출마다 수수료 일정이 있다.
- A: 60일은 지정 요청 응답 기간이다. rolling 제출 기한이 아니다.
- B: 섹션별 시계는 없다. 목표일은 완전 제출 기준이다.
- C: 첫 섹션 제출로 PDUFA 시계가 시작하지 않는다.

### Q14 [A][Domain II] — rolling submission scenario
A Breakthrough-designated sponsor agreed a rolling submission with FDA. It submitted the CMC and nonclinical modules in March and the final clinical module in July. The application is an NME NDA. From which event is the review goal calculated?

C. From the date FDA granted Breakthrough, because the designation already started the expedited review.
D. From the completion of the application in July, with the goal counted from the filing date that follows.

**정답:** D

**해설**

- A: 첫 모듈 제출은 심사 시작을 앞당길 뿐 목표일 계산 기준이 아니다.
- B: 부분 제출을 평균하는 규칙은 없다.
- C: 지정 시점은 심사 목표일과 무관하다.

### Q15 [A][Domain IV] — Phase 2 signal in a fatal disease
A sponsor's Phase 2 study shows a 40% improvement in a validated clinical endpoint versus standard of care in a fatal disease. The EOP2 meeting has not yet occurred. Which step should come first?

A. Request priority review now, because the review designation is decided during the IND stage.

### Q17 [R][Domain I] — RMAT criteria
Which criteria apply to Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designation under FD&C 506(g)?

A. A regenerative medicine therapy for a serious condition, with preliminary clinical evidence of potential to address an unmet need.
B. Any biologic for a rare disease, with nonclinical data showing a plausible mechanism of action.
C. A cell therapy for a serious condition, with completed Phase 3 data on a surrogate reasonably likely to predict benefit.
D. A regenerative medicine therapy for a serious condition, with preliminary clinical evidence of substantial improvement over available therapy.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: RMAT(506(g))는 **regenerative medicine therapy**(세포치료·조직공학·HCT/P·그 복합) + serious condition + **preliminary clinical evidence가 unmet need 대응 잠재력**을 시사해야 한다.
- B: RMAT는 희귀질환 생물의약품 전반이 아니고, 임상 증거가 필요하다.
- C: 완료된 Phase 3와 surrogate는 accelerated approval 쪽 개념이다.
- D: 기존 치료 대비 substantial improvement는 BTD 요건이다. RMAT는 그 비교를 요구하지 않는다.

### Q18 [A][Domain IV] — BTD or RMAT for a cell therapy
A cell therapy sponsor has early clinical data in a serious condition. The data suggest the product may address an unmet need, but there is no head-to-head comparison with available therapy. Which advice fits?

A. Request Fast Track first, because RMAT may be requested only after Fast Track has been granted.
B. Request neither, because both designations require evidence of improvement over available therapy.
C. Request RMAT, because it requires preliminary clinical evidence of potential without comparison to available therapy.
D. Request Breakthrough, because RMAT applies only once a confirmatory registry has been agreed with FDA.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: RMAT는 예비 임상 증거로 **unmet need 대응 잠재력**만 보이면 되고 기존 치료 대비 비교가 필요 없다. 문턱이 BTD보다 낮고 재생의학 제품은 RMAT가 기본이다.
- A: RMAT 요청에 Fast Track 선행 요건은 없다.
- B: 기존 치료 대비 개선은 BTD만 요구한다.
- D: 확증 등록부 합의는 RMAT 요청 조건이 아니다. 506(g)(7)은 AA 확증 수단의 유연성이다.

### Q19 [R][Domain I] — what RMAT adds
Compared with Breakthrough designation, what additional flexibility does RMAT designation bring?

A. Automatic priority review for the marketing application, with the goal set at 6 months from receipt.
B. Confirmation of benefit after accelerated approval through clinical evidence, patient registries or real-world evidence.
C. Approval of the product on a single adequate study without the substantial evidence standard of 505(d).
D. Exemption from the IND requirement for the clinical studies that support the designation request.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: RMAT는 BTD 수준 혜택에 더해 **506(g)(7)** 로 AA 확증을 **임상 증거·임상 연구·환자 등록부·RWE**로 충족할 수 있다.
- A: RMAT는 priority 적격성을 주지만 priority review를 자동으로 주지 않는다.
- C: 지정은 505(d) 기준을 바꾸지 않는다.
- D: IND 면제는 교과서상 RMAT 혜택이 아니다. RMAT 요청은 보통 IND 단계에서 한다.

### Q20 [An][Domain I] — RMAT versus Breakthrough
Which statement comparing RMAT and Breakthrough Therapy designation is NOT correct?

A. RMAT carries benefits at the level of Breakthrough, including intensive FDA guidance.
B. RMAT requires substantial improvement over available therapy, as Breakthrough does.
C. FDA responds to a request for either designation within 60 calendar days.

**정답:** B

- A: RMAT 혜택은 BTD 수준이다.
- C: 두 지정 모두 응답은 60 calendar days다.
- D: 둘 다 serious condition과 preliminary clinical evidence가 필요하다.

### Q21 [A][Domain I] — RMAT timing and product scope
A sponsor developing a tissue-engineered product for a serious condition has preliminary clinical data under its IND. When is RMAT usually requested, and what response time applies?

A. Usually after Phase 3, with an FDA response within the 6-month priority review.
B. Usually with the BLA, with an FDA response at the filing review on Day 60.
C. Usually at the IND stage, with an FDA response within 60 calendar days.
D. Usually after the pre-BLA meeting, with an FDA response within 90 calendar days.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: RMAT는 보통 **IND 단계**에서 요청하고 FDA는 **60 calendar days** 안에 응답한다. tissue engineering 제품은 regenerative medicine therapy에 든다.
