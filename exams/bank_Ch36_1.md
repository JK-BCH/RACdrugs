### Q01 [R][Domain I] — three gates
Which statement correctly separates the three "gates" a new medicine passes before patients can routinely obtain it?

A. Approval judges quality, safety and efficacy; coverage decides who pays; pricing sets how much is paid.
B. Approval judges quality, safety and cost-effectiveness; coverage and pricing then follow from the label.
C. Approval and coverage form one decision in the EU, while pricing is left to each manufacturer to set.
D. Coverage judges clinical efficacy first; approval and pricing are then negotiated together with payers.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 시장 접근은 세 관문이다. ① 승인 = 품질·안전·효능(FDA·EMA/EC·PMDA/MHLW), ② 급여·보장 = 누가 내는가, ③ 가격 = 얼마인가.
- B: 비용 효과는 승인 기준이 아니다. 비용 효과는 NICE 같은 국가 HTA가 급여 단계에서 본다.
- C: EU에서 승인은 EC, 급여·가격은 회원국(TFEU 168(7))이라 한 결정이 아니다.
- D: 효능 판단은 승인 관문의 일이고, 승인은 payer와 협상하는 대상이 아니다.

### Q02 [A][Domain III] — approval is not access
A biotech receives FDA approval and an EC decision in the same month. The CEO tells investors that revenue will now flow in both regions because the product "is approved". As regulatory lead, which correction is accurate?

A. Approval permits market entry; coverage and price are separate decisions by US payers and EU member states.
B. In the US, Medicaid coverage begins once CMS has negotiated a price for the new product under the IRA.
C. In both regions the regulator forwards its assessment to payers, who must then list the product in 90 days.
D. In the EU the EC decision also fixes the reimbursed price, so the US is the one region with an open question.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 승인은 진입 허가일 뿐이다. 미국은 CMS(Part B/D)·주 Medicaid·민간 보험·PBM이, EU는 회원국(TFEU 168(7))이 급여와 가격을 따로 정한다.
- B: Medicaid는 MDRP 참여 시 승인 약을 보장한다. IRA 협상은 승인 후 7/11년에 선정되는 일부 약 얘기다.
- C: 규제기관이 등재 의무를 만들지 않는다. 90일은 Transparency Dir의 회원국 결정 기한이고 신청 후 시계다.
- D: EC 결정은 가격을 정하지 않는다. 가격·급여는 회원국 권한이다.

### Q03 [R][Domain I] — EU reimbursement decision-maker
Who decides whether a centrally authorised medicine is reimbursed in an EU member state?

A. The HTA Coordination Group, whose vote binds member states once the JCA report has been adopted.
B. The EMA's CHMP, which issues a reimbursement opinion together with its benefit-risk opinion on the MAA.
C. The member state itself, acting through its national HTA bodies and payers, under TFEU Article 168(7).
D. The European Commission, on the basis of the joint clinical assessment report under the HTA Regulation.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: TFEU 168(7)로 보건 서비스·자원 배분은 회원국 권한이다. 급여는 국가 HTA 기구(G-BA·HAS·NICE·AIFA 등)와 보험자가 정한다.
- A: HTACG는 조정 기구이고, JCA는 "due consideration" 대상일 뿐 비구속이다.
- B: CHMP는 benefit-risk만 본다. 급여 의견을 내지 않는다.
- D: HTA Reg는 임상 평가만 공동으로 한다. EC가 급여를 정하지 않는다.

### Q04 [A][Domain I] — Japan decision-maker
A US company's Japanese affiliate has just received MHLW approval for a new medicine. Headquarters asks which body sets the price at which the product is reimbursed, and on what timeline. Which answer is correct?

Which statement about Medicare Part D is correct?

A. It was created by the MMA in 2003, began in 2006, and runs through private plans and PBMs.
B. It was created by the IRA in 2022 to cover physician-administered drugs at ASP plus 6%.

### Q08 [R][Domain I] — MDRP basic rebate
Under the Medicaid Drug Rebate Program, how is the basic rebate for a brand-name drug calculated?

A. 23.1% of WAC or WAC minus ASP, whichever of the two is smaller.
B. 13% of AMP, plus any increase in AMP above the rate of CPI-U.
C. 75% of non-FAMP, reduced to 65% after twelve years on the market.
D. 23.1% of AMP or AMP minus best price, whichever is the greater.

**정답:** D

- A: 기준은 WAC가 아니라 AMP이고, 작은 값이 아니라 큰 값이다.
- B: 13%는 제네릭 기본 비율이다.
- C: 75/65%는 IRA MFP 상한 구간이다.

### Q09 [A][Domain IV] — MDRP basic rebate calculation
A brand-name drug has a quarterly AMP of $100 per unit. During the quarter its lowest price to any eligible US commercial purchaser was $70 per unit, and its price has not risen faster than CPI-U. What is the basic Medicaid unit rebate?

A. $13, because the generic rate applies when commercial discounts exceed 23.1% of AMP.
B. $53.1, because the percentage and the best-price difference are added together.
C. $23.1, because the statutory percentage applies whenever a best price has been reported.
D. $30, since AMP minus best price exceeds 23.1% of AMP for this particular quarter.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 23.1% × $100 = $23.1, AMP − best price = $100 − $70 = $30. 큰 값인 $30이 기본 리베이트다. CPI-U 초과 인상이 없으므로 인플레이션 추가분은 없다.
- A: 13%는 제네릭 비율이다. 브랜드에 적용되지 않는다.
- B: 두 값을 더하지 않고 큰 값 하나를 고른다.
- C: 두 값 중 큰 값을 쓴다. 비율이 우선하는 규칙은 없다.

### Q10 [A][Domain III] — commercial discount and best price
A manufacturer plans a deep discount to a large US commercial insurer. Finance asks whether the discount has any consequence for Medicaid. Which statement is correct?

A. Best price is the lowest price in the ERP basket, so a domestic discount has little effect.

**정답:** B

**해설**

- D: 상업 할인은 best price 제외 대상이 아니다. 제외는 340B·VA·Part D 등이다.

### Q11 [An][Domain III] — VBP multiple best price rule
Why did the 2022 value-based purchasing (VBP) multiple best price rule matter to manufacturers designing outcomes-based contracts?

A. It caps the Medicaid rebate at 100% of AMP whenever an outcomes-based contract triggers a refund.
B. Outcome-dependent prices can be reported as multiple best prices, so one refund does not set best price.
C. It moves outcomes-based contracts out of Medicaid and into the CGT Access Model for participating states.

**정답:** B

**해설**

### Q14 [A][Domain IV] — IRA clock calculation
A small-molecule drug was approved in 2019 for a common (non-orphan) indication and still has no generic competition. It is among the highest Medicare Part D spenders. When is it first eligible for selection, and when would its maximum fair price apply?

A. Selection in 2026, with the negotiated price applying from 2027.
B. Selection in 2028, with the negotiated price applying from 2030.
C. Selection in 2026, with the negotiated price applying from 2028.
D. Selection in 2030, with the negotiated price applying from 2032.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: 소분자는 승인 후 7년에 선정, 9년에 적용이다. 2019 + 7 = 2026 선정, 2019 + 9 = 2028 적용이고, 선정 2년 뒤 1/1 적용 규칙과도 맞는다.
- A: 적용은 선정 2년 뒤다. 1년 뒤가 아니다.
- B: 9년을 선정 시점으로 잘못 썼다.
- D: 생물의약품의 11/13년 시계를 적용했다.

### Q15 [A][Domain IV] — OBBBA orphan exclusion
A small molecule was approved in 2019 for an orphan indication and in 2023 for a common indication; its Part D spend is $4 billion. Under the IRA as amended by OBBBA (2025-07-04), what is its negotiation exposure?

A. It loses the orphan exclusion; its clock runs from 2023, so selection is possible in 2030 and pricing in 2032.
B. It loses the exclusion; its clock runs from 2023 at the biologic pace, so selection is in 2034 and pricing in 2036.
C. It keeps the orphan exclusion, because OBBBA protects drugs that hold more than one orphan designation.
D. It loses the orphan exclusion; its clock runs from 2019, so selection was possible in 2026 and pricing in 2028.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 2023 비희귀 승인으로 orphan 제외는 사라진다. OBBBA로 시계가 첫 비희귀 승인부터 돈다. 2023 + 7 = 2030 선정, 2023 + 9 = 2032 적용이다.
- B: 소분자에는 소분자 시계(7/9년)를 쓴다. 생물의약품 시계는 해당하지 않는다.
- C: OBBBA는 비희귀 승인이 없을 때만 복수 희귀 지정 약을 보호한다.
- D: 2019 기준 2026 선정·2028 적용은 OBBBA 전 계산이다.

### Q16 [An][Domain I] — the "pill penalty"
Analysts say the IRA's 9-year/13-year split creates a "pill penalty". What strategic effect follows most directly from this split?

A. Orphan small molecules lose their exclusion once a second orphan designation is approved, even after OBBBA.

C. Small molecules gain a longer pre-negotiation window, so sponsors shift investment toward oral products.
D. For the same target, biologics become favoured and late indication expansion of small molecules loses value.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 소분자는 승인 9년, 생물의약품은 13년 뒤 협상 노출이다. 같은 타깃이면 생물의약품이 유리하고, 소분자의 후기 적응증 투자는 회수 기간이 짧아진다.
- A: OBBBA는 복수 희귀 지정도 비희귀 승인이 없으면 제외로 인정한다.
- B: 시계는 승인일부터 돈다(OBBBA 후 orphan만 첫 비희귀 승인일). 적응증마다 다시 시작하지 않는다.
- C: 방향이 반대다. 소분자의 협상 전 기간이 더 짧다.

### Q17 [R][Domain V] — IRA annual negotiation calendar
Which sequence matches the annual IRA negotiation calendar for a drug selected in a given cycle?

A. Selection list by 1 February, CMS initial offer by 1 June, MFP published by 31 October, price applied on 1 January three years later.
B. Selection list by 1 February, CMS initial offer by 1 June, MFP published by 30 November, price applied on 1 January two years later.
C. Selection list by 1 February, CMS initial offer by 1 March, MFP published by 31 October, price applied on 1 January the next year.

### Q18 [A][Domain V] — third cycle on the reference date
