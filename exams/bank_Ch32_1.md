### Q01 [R][Domain I] — 501(a)(2)(B) adulteration
Under the FD&C Act, when is a drug deemed adulterated for CGMP reasons?

A. When FDA testing of a retained sample finds a quality defect that is traced to a CGMP failure at the site.
B. When a Warning Letter has been issued and the firm fails to respond within the 15-working-day window.
C. When manufacturing methods, facilities or controls do not conform to CGMP, with no defect needing proof.
D. When the firm refuses to permit an inspection, which is the sole statutory route to CGMP adulteration.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: 501(a)(2)(B)는 제조·가공·포장·보관의 방법·시설·관리가 CGMP에 부합하지 않으면 adulterated로 본다. 실제 결함 증명은 필요 없다.
- A: 결함 입증은 요건이 아니다. 1962 Kefauver-Harris 이후의 예방 기준이다.
- B: WL 무응답은 adulteration의 정의가 아니다. WL은 사다리 중간의 행정 조치다.
- D: 사찰 거부는 501(j)라는 별도 사유이고, 501(a)(2)(B)와 나란히 있는 경로다.

### Q02 [A][Domain III] — 501(j) delay and limitation
FDA investigators arrive at a US drug plant. The site manager keeps them waiting in the lobby for two days and then limits their access to the laboratory. No product defect has been identified. What is the legal consequence?

A. Drugs from the site may be deemed adulterated under 501(j), as delaying or limiting inspection is a ground.
B. FDA must first obtain a court warrant, since section 704 requires the firm's consent for laboratory areas.
C. None until FDA identifies a CGMP deviation, because adulteration under 501(a)(2)(B) needs an observed failure.
D. The firm receives a Form 483 noting the delay, which it must answer within 15 business days before any action.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 501(j)(FDASIA §707)는 사찰 거부·지연·제한·방해를 그 자체로 adulterated 사유로 둔다. 결함 확인이 필요 없다.
- B: 704는 합리적 시간·방식의 사찰 권한을 준다. 회사 동의를 요건으로 두지 않는다.
- C: 501(j)는 501(a)(2)(B)와 별도 사유다. CGMP 이탈 관찰을 기다리지 않는다.
- D: 483은 관찰사항 목록이다. 지연의 법적 효과는 501(j)에서 나온다.

### Q03 [An][Domain IV] — passing batches and the response
After an OAI inspection, a firm's executives argue that each batch passed release testing, so the products cannot be adulterated and the response should focus on test data. Evaluate the argument.

A. Flawed — passing batches are adulterated because an OAI result creates a presumption under 501(j).
B. Sound for EU supply, since Art 116 acts on product defects, but flawed in the US under 501(a)(2)(B).
C. Sound — passing release specifications is the statutory test, so batch data rebut the CGMP finding.
D. Flawed — 501(a)(2)(B) judges methods and controls, so the reply must show how the system was fixed.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 501(a)(2)(B)는 결과가 아니라 방법·시설·관리를 본다. 시험은 샘플링이라 결함을 다 잡지 못한다(예방 기준). 응답은 "제품은 괜찮았다"가 아니라 통제가 왜 실패했고 어떻게 바꿨는지(Q10 CAPA 효과성)를 보여야 한다.
- A: 501(j)는 사찰 거부·지연 사유다. OAI 분류가 501(j) 추정을 만들지 않는다.
- B: EU Art 116도 "제조가 MA 기재·GMP와 다르게 이뤄짐"을 독립 사유로 둔다. 두 지역의 논리가 같다.
- C: 규격 합격은 법적 기준이 아니다. 합격 배치도 압류·회수 대상이 된다.

### Q04 [R][Domain I] — records in advance of inspection
Which statutory provision lets FDA request records from a drug manufacturer in advance of or in lieu of an inspection?

A. Section 704(a)(4), added by FDASIA §706.
B. Section 501(j), added by FDASIA §707.

D. Section 510(h), as amended by FDASIA §705.

**정답:** A

**해설**

- C: 303(a)는 형사 책임이고, Park doctrine은 임원 개인 책임 법리다.
- D: FDASIA §705는 위험 기반 사찰 주기에 관한 조항이다.

### Q05 [A][Domain V] — inspection tracker and FDASIA §705
A regulatory associate maintains a site inspection tracker. It schedules the next FDA surveillance inspection of each US drug site exactly two years after the last one, citing the statute. What correction should be made?

A. Replace it with a five-year cycle, the default interval for sites last classified VAI.
B. Keep two-year intervals for domestic sites, while foreign sites follow a risk-based schedule.
C. Track pre-approval inspections instead, since surveillance follows a fixed CPGM 7356.002 calendar.
D. Replace the fixed date: FDASIA §705 replaced the two-year cycle with risk-based scheduling.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: FDASIA §705가 2년 주기를 폐지하고 위험 기반 주기(이력·제품 위험·사찰 공백)를 도입했다. 고정 날짜로 추적하면 틀린다.
- A: 5년 기본 주기는 없다. 5년 공백은 오히려 우선순위를 올리는 요소다.
- B: 2년 주기는 국내 사이트에 대해서도 폐지됐다.
- C: 7356.002는 6-system 사찰 범위를 정한다. 고정 달력을 정하지 않는다.

A US company buys an active pharmaceutical ingredient from a contract API maker. During supplier qualification, a colleague says the API plant must follow 21 CFR 211 line by line. Which statement is correct?

- B: Q7은 EU Part II이면서 FDA guidance이기도 하다. 211 직접 적용도 틀렸다.
- D: 600–680은 생물의약품 규정이다.

### Q08 [A][Domain I] — Phase 1 exemption
A sponsor is manufacturing a small-molecule drug for its first Phase 1 trial and plans a Phase 2 trial next year. How does 21 CFR 211 apply across these stages?

### Q20 [A][Domain III] — Articles 116–118
An NCA inspection finds serious GMP failures at a site that holds an MIA and makes nationally authorised products. The authority wants to stop the site from manufacturing and also stop supply of one product already on the market. Which Directive 2001/83/EC articles fit these two actions?

### Q21 [An][Domain I] — why the EU has certificates and a QP
Why does the EU system rely on GMP certificates and a Qualified Person, while the US uses neither?

A. The EU has no statutory concept like adulteration, so certificates and a QP fill the legal gap in Directive 2001/83/EC.
B. EU certificates replace routine inspections, whereas the US relies on inspections, so the two do not need the same tools.
C. In the US, FDA releases each batch itself, so a separate person or certificate would duplicate the agency's signature.
D. EU: a product-release model with a QP and certificates shared by 27 Member States. US: one inspection-centred authority.
