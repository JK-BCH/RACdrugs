### Q01 [A][Domain IV] — Warning Letter and a pending ANDA
A US finished-dose site receives a Warning Letter for CGMP violations and is classified OAI. The same site is named in your pending ANDA. What is the most likely effect on the application?

A. The ANDA proceeds normally, because a Warning Letter concerns marketed products and does not affect pending applications.
B. The ANDA is approved with a postmarketing commitment requiring the site to complete its corrective actions within a set period.
C. Approval is withheld while the site remains OAI; the ANDA can stay under review but cannot be approved until the status is resolved.
D. The ANDA is refused to file, and the applicant must resubmit it once the Warning Letter has been formally closed out by FDA in writing.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: OAI 사이트가 포함된 pending 신청은 **승인 보류**된다. 접수와 심사는 계속될 수 있지만 시설 미적합으로 승인되지 않는다(ANDA는 "facility inadequate" CR).
- A: WL은 공개 경고이면서 pending 신청의 승인 보류로 이어진다. 시판 제품에 한정된 효과가 아니다.
- B: 시설 OAI 상태를 시판 후 약속으로 대신해 승인하는 경로는 교과서에 없다.
- D: 신청이 접수 거부되는 것은 아니다. 심사는 진행되고 승인만 막힌다.

### Q02 [R][Domain III] — what closes a Warning Letter
Under the policy FDA introduced in 2009, when is a Warning Letter close-out letter issued?

A. When two years pass without a new Form 483 at the site, at which point the letter is withdrawn from the FDA website.
B. When FDA has verified, usually through a follow-up inspection, that the violations cited in the letter have been corrected.
C. When an independent expert certifies CGMP compliance and the court supervising the firm approves the certification.
D. When the firm submits its written response within 15 working days and FDA finds the planned actions adequate on paper.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: close-out letter(2009)는 FDA가 WL 항목의 **교정을 확인**했을 때 발행된다. 확인은 보통 follow-up 사찰로 하고, VAI/NAI 재분류 뒤 승인이 재개된다.
- A: 기간 경과로 자동 종결되는 규정은 없다.
- C: 독립 전문가 인증과 법원 승인은 consent decree의 재가동 조건이다.
- D: 기한 내 서면 응답은 출발점일 뿐이다. 계획만으로는 종결되지 않는다.

### Q03 [A][Domain V] — Warning Letter response deadline
A Warning Letter dated Monday 3 August 2026 asks for a response within 15 working days. Counting from the day after the letter and assuming no federal holiday falls in the period, by when must the response arrive?

A. Friday 21 August 2026, counting 15 working days with the letter date itself included.
B. Tuesday 18 August 2026, counting 15 calendar days from the date of the letter.
C. Monday 24 August 2026, counting 15 working days from the date of the letter.
D. Thursday 3 September 2026, counting 30 calendar days from the date of the letter.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: 다음 날(8월 4일, 화)부터 영업일을 센다. 8/4–8/7이 4일, 8/10–8/14가 9일, 8/17–8/21이 14일, 8/24(월)이 15번째다.
- A: 서한 날짜를 첫날로 넣어 하루 일찍 끝냈다. 다음 날부터 센다.
- B: 달력일 15일로 센 날짜다. WL 기한은 working days다.
- D: WL 응답 기한은 15 working days다. 30일 기준은 근거가 없다.

### Q04 [A][Domain III] — Form 483 response window
An FDA investigator issues a Form 483 at the close-out meeting on Friday 6 March 2026. The firm wants its response to be considered before FDA decides whether to issue a Warning Letter. Assuming no holidays, what is the last day to submit?

A. Saturday 21 March 2026, because the window is 15 calendar days.
B. Monday 23 March 2026, because weekend deadlines roll to Monday.
C. Monday 6 April 2026, because the window is one calendar month.
D. Friday 27 March 2026, because the window is 15 business days.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 483 응답 창은 **15 business days**(2009 FR)다. 3/9–3/13이 5일, 3/16–3/20이 10일, 3/23–3/27이 15일이라 3월 27일(금)이 마지막 날이다.
- A: 달력일 15일로 센 날이다. business days와 calendar days를 혼동했다.
- B: 달력일로 센 뒤 주말을 넘긴 것이다. 기준 자체가 business days다.
- C: 한 달 기준은 근거가 없다.

### Q05 [R][Domain III] — business days versus working days
Which statement about the US response windows for a Form 483 and a Warning Letter is correct?

A. Both are 15 days counted in business (working) days; the common error is counting them as calendar days.
B. The Form 483 window is 15 calendar days, while the Warning Letter window is 15 working days from the letter.
C. The Form 483 window is set by regulation in 21 CFR 211, while the Warning Letter window is set by the FD&C Act.
D. The Form 483 window is 15 business days, while the Warning Letter window is 15 calendar days from receipt.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 483은 **15 business days**, WL은 **15 working days**다. business days와 working days는 같은 뜻(영업일)이고, 함정은 달력일로 세는 것이다.
- B·D: 둘 중 하나를 달력일로 바꾼 오답이다. 둘 다 영업일 기준이다.
- C: 483 응답 창은 2009 FR 고지에서 나왔다. 211 조문이 아니다.

### Q06 [An][Domain III] — why the 483 deadline matters
A firm plans to send a thorough Form 483 response well after the 483 response window, arguing that a more complete package will impress FDA. What is the main weakness of this plan?

A. FDA considers responses received within 15 business days before deciding on a Warning Letter; a late one may not be.
B. A late response converts the observations into a Warning Letter, because each 483 item carries a formal grade.
C. A late response voids the Form 483, so FDA must re-inspect the site before any classification can be made.
D. A late response is treated as a refusal of inspection under 501(j), which makes the products adulterated by itself under the Act.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 2009 FR 고지에 따라 **15 business days 안의 응답**은 WL 결정 전에 검토된다. 늦으면 WL 판단에 반영되지 않을 수 있으니, 기한 안에 내고 이후 **업데이트 응답**으로 보완한다.
- B: 개별 483 항목엔 공식 등급이 없고, 늦은 응답이 WL로 자동 전환되지도 않는다.
- C: 483은 관찰사항 목록이다. 응답이 늦어도 무효가 되지 않는다.
- D: 501(j)는 사찰 거부·지연·제한·방해다. 483 응답 지연과 다르다.

### Q07 [A][Domain II] — CR letter citing a CMO's OAI status
Your pending BLA receives a Complete Response letter citing only the OAI status of your contract manufacturer. The product data are not questioned. Which statement about the two options is correct?

A. Fixing the CMO shifts legal responsibility for the CGMP failure to the CMO, so the applicant carries less risk from here.
B. Switching sites avoids any new inspection, because the product data already support approval of the BLA.

### Q08 [A][Domain II] — resubmission clock after a facility CR

A Korean finished-dose plant is placed on Import Alert 66-40 after an OAI inspection. A shipment from the plant arrives at a US port the following week. What happens to it?

A. It is seized by US Marshals under section 304, and the importer must contest the seizure in federal district court.

A. Ask the importer of record to certify each shipment as compliant, which removes the plant from the alert after six months of clean entries.

### Q14 [R][Domain III] — consent decree terms
Which set of terms is typical of an FDA consent decree of permanent injunction for repeated CGMP violations?

A. A one-time civil money penalty under the drug CGMP provisions, after which the firm returns to routine surveillance inspection.
B. A mandatory recall order for every marketed batch, issued by FDA under its drug recall authority without a court decision.

### Q15 [An][Domain III] — where the consent decree sits
A board member asks why FDA would seek a consent decree rather than another Warning Letter for a firm with three Warning Letters in five years. Which explanation fits the enforcement ladder?

A. A consent decree replaces criminal prosecution under 303, so executives named in it cannot later be held personally liable for the same acts.
B. A consent decree is an administrative letter that carries more weight than a Warning Letter but has the same legal basis.
C. A consent decree puts compliance under a court order, so further violations carry court-ordered consequences such as damages.
D. A consent decree is required whenever a firm is classified OAI, so the number of earlier Warning Letters is not relevant.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: WL은 행정 서한이다. 반복 위반·WL 무시 뒤엔 302 금지명령 소송의 합의로 **법원 명령**이 생기고, 위반하면 liquidated damages 같은 법원 명령상 결과가 따른다.
- A: consent decree는 303 형사책임을 없애지 않는다. Park doctrine은 그대로다.
- B: WL과 달리 consent decree는 법원 명령이다. 같은 법적 근거가 아니다.
- D: OAI는 규제 조치 권고일 뿐이다. 모든 OAI가 consent decree로 가지 않는다.

### Q16 [A][Domain IV] — CAP site with a non-compliance statement
A national inspection of a site making a centrally authorised product finds critical deficiencies, and the NCA issues a GMP non-compliance statement. Which EU-level step follows for the marketing authorisation itself?

A. The MAH must withdraw the authorisation within 90 days, because a non-compliance statement cancels any GMP certificate.
B. The supervisory NCA suspends the centralised marketing authorisation directly under Article 118 of Directive 2001/83.
C. EMA and the CHMP assess benefit-risk and supply need, and the Commission may act under Article 20 of Regulation 726/2004.

**정답:** C

**해설**

- B: Art 118은 사찰국의 **MIA** 조치다. CAP의 MA 조치는 Art 20이다.
- D: CAP은 중앙 허가라 회원국별 국가 허가로 처리하지 않는다.

### Q18 [R][Domain III] — Articles 116, 117 and 118
Which mapping of Directive 2001/83 enforcement articles is correct?

A. Art 116 suspends the manufacturing authorisation; Art 117 suspends the MA; Art 118 recalls batches from the market.
B. Art 116 recalls batches; Art 117 revokes the MIA; Art 118 lets EMA suspend centralised authorisations directly.
C. Art 116 issues the GMP certificate; Art 117 issues the non-compliance statement; Art 118 publishes both in EudraGMDP.
D. Art 116 suspends or revokes the MA; Art 117 prohibits supply and recalls; Art 118 suspends the manufacturing authorisation.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: **116** MA 정지·철회·변경, **117** 공급 금지·회수, **118** MIA 정지·철회다.

### Q61 [A][Domain V] — sequencing deadlines after an inspection
