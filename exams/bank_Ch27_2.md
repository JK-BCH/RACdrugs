### Q01 [R][Domain II] — RTF grounds under 314.101(d)
Which of the following is a ground on which FDA may refuse to file an NDA under 21 CFR 314.101(d)?

A. The sponsor has not yet scheduled the pre-approval inspection with the field office for its plant.
B. The proposed proprietary name is still under review by the FDA division that handles name safety.
C. The applicant plans a risk management plan in the EU but has not shared it with FDA in Module 1.
D. The application omits required sections such as the integrated summaries or case report forms.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 314.101(d)의 RTF 사유는 ① 불완전(필수 섹션·CRF·ISS/ISE·데이터 누락) ② 형식 불량 ③ 명백히 승인 불가 ④ 수수료 미납이다. 필수 섹션 누락은 ①이다.
- A: 사찰 일정은 신청자가 dossier에 채워 넣는 완결성 요건이 아니다. RTF 사유 목록에 없다.
- B: 이름 검토 진행 중이라는 사정은 교과서의 RTF 사유 목록에 없다.
- C: RMP(1.8.2)는 EU Module 1 요건이다. 미국 RTF 판단 대상이 아니다.

### Q02 [A][Domain II] — ISS missing at submission
A sponsor submits an NDA in valid eCTD format. The clinical section contains individual study reports, but no integrated summary of safety was compiled, and the cover letter says one will follow after submission. What is the most likely regulatory outcome?

A. FDA accepts the NDA because valid eCTD format satisfies 314.101(d), leaving content completeness to the later review cycle.
B. FDA files the NDA and lists the gap in the 74-day letter, since an integrated safety summary is optional for single-indication products.
C. FDA files the NDA but suspends the PDUFA goal date until the safety summary arrives, then restarts the review clock from that date.
D. FDA may refuse to file during the 60-day filing review, because the application is incomplete, and the PDUFA clock does not start.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: ISS(314.50(d)(5)(vi)(a))가 빠진 신청은 314.101(d)의 **불완전** 사유에 해당한다. 결정은 **60일 filing review**에서 하고, RTF면 PDUFA 시계가 시작되지 않는다.
- A: eCTD 형식은 ② 형식 요건만 충족한다. ① 불완전은 별도 사유다.
- B: ISS는 314.50(d)(5)(vi)(a)의 요건이다. 적응증 수에 따라 선택이 되지 않는다.
- C: filed 상태에서 목표일을 멈추는 장치는 교과서에 없다. 불완전 신청의 처리는 RTF다.

### Q03 [R][Domain II] — timing and effect of RTF
When is a refuse-to-file decision on an NDA made, and what is its effect on the review clock?

A. Within the 74-day period; the PDUFA clock keeps running while the sponsor corrects the gaps.
B. At the mid-cycle meeting; the review clock pauses and resumes once the dossier is repaired.
C. Within the 60-day filing review; the PDUFA clock does not start for a refused application.
D. Within the 60-day filing review; the PDUFA goal date is extended by 3 months instead.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: RTF는 접수 후 **60일 filing review**에서 결정되고, RTF면 **PDUFA 시계 미시작**이다(314.101(d)).
- A: 74일은 filed 상태의 쟁점 통지(74-day letter) 시점이다. RTF 결정 시점이 아니다.
- B: RTF는 filing 단계 결정이다. 심사 중반 회의와 무관하다.
- D: 3개월 연장은 filed 이후 **major amendment**의 효과다. RTF의 효과가 아니다.

### Q04 [A][Domain II] — 74-day letter: ISE and define.xml
FDA's 74-day letter states: "No integrated summary of effectiveness; datasets for Study 301 lack define.xml." The letter does not refuse to file the application. What is the correct reading and response?

A. The NDA is on hold; the review clock stops until the sponsor submits the ISE and define.xml, then restarts from day zero.
B. The NDA has been refused; the sponsor must withdraw, add the ISE and define.xml, and resubmit as a new application with a new fee.
C. The NDA is filed; submit the ISE and define.xml as an amendment by the deadline; a major amendment may extend the goal date.
D. The NDA is filed; the gaps are advisory, so the sponsor may answer them in the first annual report after approval.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: 74-day letter는 **filed 상태에서 filing 쟁점 통지**다. ISE(314.50(d)(5)(v))와 define.xml을 지정 기한 내 **amendment**로 내고, 그것이 **major amendment**면 목표일이 3개월 연장될 수 있다.
- A: 시계를 처음으로 되돌리는 장치는 교과서에 없다. 효과는 major amendment의 3개월 연장이다.
- B: 74-day letter는 RTF가 아니다(흔한 혼동 9). 재신청 대상이 아니다.
- D: ISE는 314.50(d)(5)(v) 요건이다. 시판 후 annual report로 미룰 사안이 아니다.

### Q05 [A][Domain I] — goal date after a major amendment
An NDA has a PDUFA goal date of 2026-08-14. In response to the 74-day letter, the sponsor submits a new integrated efficacy analysis that FDA classifies as a major amendment. If FDA applies the extension described in the textbook, what is the new goal date?

**정답:** C

**해설**

- B: 6개월 연장으로 계산했다. 교과서 값은 3개월이다.
- D: 1개월 연장으로 계산했다.

### Q06 [R][Domain II] — 314.50(f)(2) mandatory CRFs
Under 21 CFR 314.50(f)(2), for which subjects must full case report forms be submitted with an NDA without waiting for an FDA request?

A. Subjects who died and subjects who discontinued because of an adverse event.

### Q07 [A][Domain II] — CRF package check
An NDA's Module 5.3.7 contains SDTM/ADaM datasets for every subject in all studies and full CRFs for the 4 subjects who died. Another 12 subjects discontinued because of adverse events; their CRFs were not included. What should regulatory affairs advise?

A. The package is complete, because datasets for each subject replace the need for any individual CRFs under 314.50(f).
B. Include CRFs for each enrolled subject, because 314.50(f)(2) requires complete CRFs whenever a death occurs in a study.
C. Provide the 12 CRFs if FDA asks in the 74-day letter, because discontinuation CRFs are a post-filing review item.
D. Add the CRFs of the 12 subjects who discontinued for adverse events; other CRFs are supplied on FDA request.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: (f)(1) 표(tabulations)는 datasets로 충족되지만, (f)(2)는 **사망 + AE 중단** 피험자의 CRF를 필수로 한다. AE 중단 12명의 CRF가 빠졌다. 나머지는 요청 시다.
- A: datasets는 (f)(1)을 충족한다. (f)(2)의 CRF 원본 요건은 별개다.
- B: 사망이 있다고 전원 CRF가 필수가 되지 않는다. 필수 범주는 사망·AE 중단 피험자다.
- C: AE 중단 CRF는 제출 시 필수다. 빠지면 RTF의 불완전 사유가 될 수 있다.

### Q09 [An][Domain II] — why the US asks for more clinical depth
A global team asks why FDA requires integrated summaries and individual CRFs when the EU accepts Module 2.7 summaries and requests CRFs only if needed. Which explanation is best?

A. ICH M4 harmonised the content of Module 5 on the US model, and the EU later opted out of the integrated summary sections.
B. FDA reanalyses the raw data itself, so integrated analyses and death/dropout CRFs let it verify safety signals on its own.
C. FDA lacks the staff to read study reports, so it asks for integrated summaries that replace the individual reports in Module 5.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: 미국은 **FDA가 원데이터를 직접 재분석**(datasets·SAS)하는 전통이라 ISS/ISE와 사망·중단 CRF로 자체 검증한다. EU는 **rapporteur가 신청자의 요약·보고서를 평가**하고 필요 시 요청한다.
- A: CTD는 형식만 조화했다. 내용 요건은 지역마다 다르다(흔한 혼동 1).
- C: 통합 요약은 개별 보고서를 대체하지 않는다. 5.3.5.3은 추가 분석이다.

### Q10 [A][Domain IV] — late ClinicalTrials.gov registration
During NDA assembly, regulatory affairs learns that the pivotal trial was registered on ClinicalTrials.gov later than required. The draft Form FDA 3674 states full compliance. What is the appropriate action?

A. Move the issue to the clinical overview in 2.5, because Form 3674 covers trials that are still ongoing at filing.
B. Record the true registration status on the form; a false certification can bring civil or criminal liability.
C. Sign the form as drafted, since registration timing is reviewed by NIH and does not affect the NDA content.
D. Omit Form 3674, since a late registration makes the certification inapplicable to this application.

**정답:** B

**해설**

- D: 양식을 빼면 314.50 요건이 빠지는 것이다. RTF 위험만 커진다.

### Q17 [R][Domain I] — ANDA refuse-to-receive standard
Under the GDUFA refuse-to-receive standards, when does FDA refuse to receive an ANDA?

A. When it has 1 or more major deficiencies, or 10 or more minor deficiencies.
B. When it has 3 or more major deficiencies, or 5 or more minor deficiencies of any kind.
C. When it has 1 or more major deficiencies; minor deficiencies are handled in review.
D. When it has more than 10 minor deficiencies, or 2 or more major deficiencies.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: ANDA가 **substantially complete**하지 않으면 RTR이다. 기준은 **major 1개 이상** 또는 **minor 10개 이상**이다.
- B: 숫자를 바꿔 놓았다. major는 하나만 있어도 RTR이다.
- C: minor도 개수 기준이 있다. 10개 이상이면 RTR이다.
- D: "10개 초과"로 쓰면 정확히 10개에서 결론이 뒤바뀐다. 기준은 10개 이상이고, major는 1개면 충분하다.
