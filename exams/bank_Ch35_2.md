### Q01 [A][Domain III] — sample left before any request
During a visit in Germany, a sales representative leaves two packs of a new prescription product with a physician who has not asked for them. He plans to collect a signed request form at the next visit. How should this be assessed under Article 96 of Directive 2001/83?

A. Acceptable, because the representative may complete the request form on the physician's behalf.
B. Not compliant, because a written, signed and dated request from the prescriber must come first.
C. Acceptable, because the signed form will be on file before the next national sample count.
D. Acceptable, provided each pack is at most the smallest presentation and carries the SmPC document.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: Art 96(1)은 샘플을 **처방 자격자의 서면·서명·날짜 요청**이 있은 뒤에만 줄 수 있게 한다. 레프가 먼저 놓고 가는 것은 순서가 틀렸다.
- A: 요청은 처방자 본인이 한다. 대리 작성은 요청 요건을 비우는 것이고, 미국 PDMA도 대리 요청을 허용하지 않는다.
- C: 요청은 공급보다 **앞서야** 한다. 나중에 받은 서명은 이미 한 공급을 정당화하지 않는다.
- D: 최소 포장·SmPC 동봉은 Art 96의 다른 조건이다. 요청 선행 조건을 대신하지 않는다.

### Q02 [R][Domain III] — Article 96 conditions
Which set of conditions reflects Article 96(1) of Directive 2001/83 for free samples of prescription medicines?

A. Given exceptionally, to prescribers and pharmacists, on oral request, in any pack size, labelled as a sample.
B. Given routinely at launch, to any HCP, on oral request, with a patient leaflet but not the SmPC enclosed.
C. Given exceptionally, to prescribers, on written request, at most the smallest pack, with the SmPC.
D. Given on written request to a prescriber, including narcotics if the national annual limit is not reached.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: Art 96(1)은 **예외적** 제공, **처방 자격자**, **서면·서명·날짜 요청**, **최소 시판 포장 이하**, "free medical sample — not for sale" 표기, **SmPC 동봉**, 연간 제한 수량을 요구한다.
- A: 구두 요청은 안 되고, 포장은 최소 시판 포장 이하다.
- B: 샘플은 일상 판촉 수단이 아니고 서면 요청이 필요하다. 동봉 문서는 SmPC다.
- D: 마약·향정신성 의약품 샘플은 수량과 무관하게 금지된다.

### Q03 [A][Domain V] — control system for samples
A company runs sample programmes in France and Germany. Its auditors find that sample requests are filed but no one can trace which batch went to which physician or check totals against national limits. Which element of the programme is missing?

A. The EFPIA Transfer of Value disclosure, because samples count as payments that must be published by 30 June.
B. The Article 91(1) essential information, which must accompany each sample pack in place of the SmPC document.
C. The Article 96(2) manufacturer control and accountability system covering records, quantities and traceability.
D. The Article 93 obligation for representatives to report adverse events heard during sample visits to the company.

**정답:** C

**해설**
- C: Art 96(2)는 샘플 공급에 대한 **제조자 통제·책임 시스템**(요청서 보관·배포 기록·수량 추적·회수 가능)을 요구한다. 실무로는 CRM 수량 카운터(국가 한도 하드 스톱)·배치 추적·감사가 여기에 해당한다.
- A: 교과서는 샘플을 ToV 공개 항목으로 다루지 않는다. 빠진 것은 기록·추적 체계다.
- B: 샘플에는 SmPC를 동봉한다. Art 91(1)은 HCP 광고의 필수 정보 조항이다.
- D: Art 93은 영업사원의 교육·SmPC 제공·보고 의무다. 배치 추적과 수량 통제의 근거가 아니다.

### Q04 [An][Domain III] — EU sample rules versus US PDMA
A global compliance lead wants one sample standard operating procedure for the EU and the US. Which statement correctly compares Article 96 and PDMA/21 CFR 203?

A. PDMA allows the sale of samples to pharmacies with records, whereas Article 96 requires the "not for sale" statement.
B. Both require a prescriber's written request and manufacturer records; the US adds a signed receipt and bans sale.
C. Article 96 lets the representative request samples for the prescriber, whereas PDMA requires the prescriber to sign.
D. Article 96 allows samples on oral request, whereas PDMA requires a written request and a signed receipt for each delivery.

**정답:** B

**해설**
- B: 두 체계 모두 **처방자 서면 요청**과 **제조자 기록**이 핵심이다. 미국 203은 여기에 **수령 확인 서명**, 재고·분실 보고, **판매·거래 금지**를 둔다(203.30).
- A: PDMA는 샘플 판매·거래를 연방 범죄로 금지한다.
- C: 대리 요청은 미국에서 불가하고, Art 96도 처방자 요청을 전제로 한다.
- D: Art 96도 서면·서명·날짜 요청을 요구한다.

### Q05 [A][Domain III] — HCO advisory board, first step
A German hospital department will act as a consultant for an advisory board, and three of its physicians will be paid. Marketing has already chosen the attendees from the top prescribers list. What is the first problem to correct?

A. The payments must be disclosed on the company website within one month of the meeting under EFPIA rules.
B. The meeting must be reviewed by the national authority before invitations are sent under Article 97.
C. The three physicians must first be registered as members of FSA before they can be paid as consultants.
D. Attendees must be chosen for expertise against a documented legitimate need, not prescribing volume.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: 자문회의는 **정당한 필요**를 먼저 문서화하고, 참석자 수가 필요에 비례하며, 참석자는 **전문성** 기준으로 고른다. 처방량 기준 선정은 마케팅 위장이고 AKS·EFPIA 양쪽에서 문제다.
- A: EFPIA ToV 공개는 **다음 해 6/30**까지다. 회의 후 단기 공개 규칙은 교과서에 없다.
- B: Art 97은 집행을 회원국에 맡기는 조항이다. 개별 회의의 당국 사전 심사 의무가 아니다.
- C: FSA는 기업 자율규제 기구다. HCP가 FSA 회원이 될 필요는 없다.

### Q06 [R][Domain III] — HCO advisory board sequence
Which sequence matches the compliance steps for engaging a German HCO and its physicians as paid advisers?

A. Legitimate need, written FMV contract, prior hospital approval, modest hospitality, ToV disclosure, kept records.
B. Legitimate need, oral agreement on fees, hospitality including partners, ToV disclosure by the following 30 June.
C. Written contract at FMV, then legitimate-need document, then ToV disclosure, then hospital approval after the meeting.
D. Hospital approval, then FMV contract, then disclosure within the same year, then legitimate-need record for audit.

**정답:** A

**해설**
- A: 순서는 **정당한 필요 문서화 → 서면 계약·FMV → 병원 사전 승인(StGB §299a/b) → 적정 접대 → ToV 공개 → 기록**이다.

- D: ToV 공개는 같은 해가 아니라 **다음 해 6/30**까지다. 정당한 필요는 사후 기록이 아니라 출발점이다.

### Q07 [A][Domain V] — ToV disclosure deadline calculation
A company paid advisory fees to a German HCO and three physicians at a meeting in 2025. One physician refused consent to individual disclosure. Under the EFPIA Code, when and how must these transfers be disclosed?

A. By 30 June 2027; the HCO and physicians aggregated as a single R&D amount, kept online for three years.
B. By 30 June 2026; the refusing physician's fee is published individually because consent is not required.
C. By 31 December 2025; the refusing physician's fee is omitted entirely, and the HCO amount is published.
D. By 30 June 2026; consenting physicians individually, the refusing one in aggregate, the HCO separately.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: EFPIA ToV는 보고 연도 종료 후 **다음 해 6/30**까지 공개한다. 2025년 지급분은 **2026-06-30**이 기한이다. HCP는 **동의 시 개인별, 미동의 시 합산**, HCO는 HCO별로 공개하고 **3년** 유지한다.
- A: 기한이 한 해 늦다. R&D 합산은 연구 관련 지급의 범주이고, 자문 보수를 그쪽으로 옮기는 근거는 없다.
- B: 개인별 공개에는 동의가 필요하다. 미동의자는 합산으로 넘어간다.
- C: 기한이 틀렸고, 미동의자도 빠지지 않고 합산에 들어간다.

### Q09 [A][Domain II] — CHMP positive opinion press release

A. Usually acceptable as factual corporate news, but advertising if it urges patients or overstates the efficacy.
B. Permitted, provided the release carries the abbreviated prescribing information required by Article 91(1).

**정답:** A

### Q12 [An][Domain I] — synchronising launch communications
Leadership wants all launch communications ready the day the EU and US approvals arrive. Which plan reflects the rules for both regions?

A. Release EU and US promotion together at the earlier approval, because one MA permits promotion in both.
B. Use the CHMP opinion date for the EU and the PDUFA date for the US, sending Form 2253 after the first quarter.
C. Clear materials via MLR in advance; EU promotion at the EC decision, US at approval, Form 2253 at first use.

**해설**

### Q13 [R][Domain III] — Article 89 mandatory content
An OTC cough medicine will be advertised on EU television. Which content is mandatory under Article 89(1)?

A. The full SmPC text, the supply classification, the price, and the date on which the advertisement was last revised.
B. Clearly an ad for a medicine, with its name, information for correct use and advice to read the leaflet.
C. The brief summary of side effects, contraindications and effectiveness, plus a toll-free number for the leaflet.
D. The name of the product, a pharmacist's endorsement, the price, and a statement that it is free of side effects.

**정답:** B

**해설**

- D: HCP 추천과 "부작용 없음"은 Art 90이 금지하는 내용이다.

### Q14 [A][Domain III] — Article 90 prohibited content
An EU OTC analgesic ad shows a well-known actress saying she trusts it because "it's natural, so it's safe," then a child taking it. How many of these elements are prohibited by Article 90?

A. None, because Article 90 covers prescription medicines advertised to the public rather than OTC.
B. The child alone, because celebrity statements are allowed if the person is not a health professional.
C. The natural-means-safe claim alone, because celebrities and children may appear with a warning.
D. All three: the celebrity endorsement, the natural-means-safe claim and the child-directed content.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: Art 90은 **유명인 추천**, **천연이라 안전하다는 암시**, **어린이 대상** 광고를 각각 금지한다. 세 요소 모두 걸린다.
- A: 처방약 공중 광고는 Art 88로 아예 금지된다. Art 90은 허용된 OTC 공중 광고의 금지 내용이다.
- B: Art 90은 과학자·HCP뿐 아니라 **유명인** 추천도 금지한다.
- C: 경고 문구로 금지 내용이 허용되지 않는다.

### Q15 [A][Domain III] — Article 90 vaccine exception
An EU public campaign for a vaccine says that not being vaccinated could harm the viewer's health. Another OTC ad says that not taking a vitamin product could worsen health. Which assessment is correct?

A. Both are acceptable, because Article 90 restricts just the claim that a medicine is free of side effects.
B. Both breach Article 90, because suggesting harm from not taking a medicine is prohibited without exception.
C. The OTC message is acceptable and the vaccine message breaches Article 88, which bars vaccine ads to the public.
D. The vaccine message can fall within the vaccination exception; the OTC message breaches Article 90.

**정답:** D

**해설**
- D: Art 90은 미복용 시 건강 악화 암시를 금지하되 **백신 캠페인은 제외**한다. Art 88(4)도 백신 캠페인을 예외로 둔다. OTC 문구는 금지 내용이다.
- A: Art 90 금지 목록은 부작용 없음 주장 외에도 여러 항목을 포함한다.
- B: 백신 예외가 있다. 모든 경우 금지가 아니다.
- C: Art 88(4)는 백신 캠페인을 금지가 아니라 **예외**로 둔다.

Which statement about reminder advertising in the EU is correct?

C. Reminder advertising for HCPs must still contain the full Article 91(1) essential information and revision date.
D. Reminder advertising is allowed for prescription medicines to the public if it names the product without the indication.
